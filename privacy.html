Privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Absolve Gaming Uploader ("the tool") uses YouTube API Services. By authorizing it, you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles your data under the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the tool accesses

  • Your channel's ID, name, handle, and profile picture, to confirm uploads go to the right channel.
  • The videos the tool uploads: their title, description, tags, visibility, processing status, and link.

The tool does not access other videos, comments, subscribers, analytics, or your Google account's other data.

How the data is stored

  • The authorization (an OAuth refresh token) is stored encrypted in a password manager vault that Absolve Gaming controls.
  • Short-lived access tokens are kept only in memory while an upload runs.
  • Upload records (video ID, link, title, and status) and a copy of the channel's profile picture are kept on Absolve Gaming's computer.

How the data is used and shared

The data is used only to upload videos and confirm the uploads. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else, with one exception. The tool is started by AI coding assistants on Absolve Gaming's computer (such as Anthropic's Claude Code and OpenAI's Codex). Those assistants see the channel name, handle, channel ID, and the titles, links, and status of uploaded videos, so they can report the results. The tool never shows them your authorization tokens.

Neither the tool nor this website uses cookies.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the tool's access at any time on your Google Account permissions page. To have the stored authorization and records deleted, open an issue on GitHub. Don't put personal details in the issue, because issues are public. Deletion happens within 7 days.
