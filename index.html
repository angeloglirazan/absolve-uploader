Absolve Gaming Uploader

Absolve Gaming Uploader is a private tool that Absolve Gaming uses to publish videos it produces to YouTube channels. It runs on Absolve Gaming's own computer. It is not a public service, and there is no sign-up.

A channel owner authorizes the tool once through Google's sign-in. After that, the tool can:

  • confirm which YouTube channel the authorization belongs to (channel ID, name, handle, and profile picture), so a video only goes to the channel that was intended;
  • upload a video with its title, description, tags, and visibility;
  • read back that video's details and processing status to confirm the upload worked.

The tool does not read other videos, comments, subscribers, analytics, or any other account data.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. Read the privacy policy and the terms of service.
